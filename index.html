Keep LuckyGem lovely

A few simple rules help make the server a place everyone can enjoy.

1

Be kind

Treat players respectfully. No harassment, hate speech, or bullying.

2

No griefing or stealing

Don't damage, take from, or alter another player's builds or items without permission.

3

Play fair

No cheats, hacked clients, or exploits that give an unfair advantage. Follow staff directions.

4

Keep chat friendly

No spam, advertising, or deliberately disruptive behaviour.

5

Build considerately

Respect community areas, shared paths, shops, and other players' space.

6

Ask if you're unsure

Check with staff before doing something that might affect another player or a community project.
