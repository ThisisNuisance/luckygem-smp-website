LuckyGem community guide

LuckyGem is a warm, whitelisted, cross-platform community for players who love building, exploring, and growing together. Please read these guidelines before applying.

🍀 Welcome to LuckyGem

Founded in 2020 by a group of YouTubers who love creativity and exploration, LuckyGem brings Java and Bedrock players together for a mostly vanilla experience, with carefully chosen additions. We value friendship, fairness, and a world that everyone can enjoy. To join, read the rules and submit an application through our Discord application channel.

Community rules

1

Respect everyone

Treat others with kindness. Racism, homophobia, transphobia, discrimination, harassment, and real-life threats are not tolerated in-game or on Discord.

2

No griefing or theft

Do not steal, grief, or damage another player's items or builds. Poor behaviour that harms the community is not acceptable.

3

Keep chat friendly

No spam, excessive caps, or disruptive behaviour. Use common sense and help keep conversations welcoming.

4

PvP needs consent

All players involved must agree before PvP begins.

5

Pranks need agreement

Pranks are allowed only when everyone involved agrees beforehand. The prankster must clean up any mess, and pranks are not allowed in public areas.

6

No exploiting bugs

Do not exploit known bugs or unintended features. If you are unsure, submit a support ticket before using them.

7

Claim your area properly

Rename a banner to your Minecraft username and place it in your chosen area. Light up your area. A Discord claim is valid only after you have done this in-game.

8

Use approved mods only

X-ray, fly hacks, kill aura/auto-aim, unnatural speed mods, and no-auto-reconnect mods are prohibited. Unsure about a mod? Ask staff through a ticket.

Dupers and redstone

🧰

Allowed duplicators

Only TNT, carpet, and rail duplicators are allowed. TNT duplicators must be turned off whenever you log out, even briefly.

⚙️

Redstone etiquette

Do not build lag machines or overly complex, constantly active contraptions. Turn off clocks and farms when you are away, and make sure your machines do not interfere with or damage others' builds.

The End

  • If you have an Elytra, fly north in the Northern Hemisphere.
  • If someone is in the End, give them a heads-up before spawning a Wither or Ender Dragon.
  • Only one public Enderman farm is allowed; it belongs next to the Obsidian Spawn Platform.
  • The End resets every three months. Public farms, including the Enderman farm, will be regenerated.

The Nether

  • Main tunnels run north, south, east, and west from coordinates 0, 0. Do not build or edit within seven blocks of these coordinates.
  • Connect your Nether portal to the nearest main tunnel. Do not create right-angle side tunnels. If a tunnel is occupied, ask its owner or staff about sharing.
  • Main tunnels may be no larger than 9 × 9 blocks, including walls, floor, and ceiling.
  • Personal tunnels and portals must follow the server layout. Place your portal at Y 115 and align it with your Overworld portal.
  • Space personal tunnels every 10 blocks. Blocks at the 0 coordinates must remain untouched Netherrack.
  • Personal portal hubs must be on the Nether roof and within your personal Overworld base area.

Spawn and shopping district

🏰

Spawn guidelines

  • Spawn's theme is fantasy; builds must match.
  • No redstone at spawn without permission.
  • Submit a ticket and get approval before building at spawn. Approved projects may be collaborative.
  • Do not build within 500 blocks in any direction of coordinates 0, 0 (a 1,000-block-wide area).
🛍️

Shopping district

  • Shop plots are 20 × 20 blocks. Ask by ticket for more space and leave 5–10 blocks between shops.
  • Explosives cannot be placed in the district, although they may be sold.
  • Run a stall for seven days as a trial before receiving approval for a shop plot. Vacant stalls are free: place a sign with your username and opening date, keep it stocked, and collect your profits.
  • After approval, restore your stall to its original condition.
  • After 30 days inactive, staff will contact you. If it remains inactive for another 30 days, the shop may be removed and stock/materials returned.
  • All sales are final; no returns.

Player bases

  • You may claim biomes, but only claim what you intend to use and leave room for neighbours. Keep bases roughly 500–1,000 blocks apart.
  • Limit of 50 villagers per player/group.
  • Limit of 15 beehives per player/group.
  • Limit of 150 animals per player/group.

Frequently asked questions

🔑 How do I join?

LuckyGem is whitelisted. Read the rules and apply through the Discord application channel. Add the word ACCEPT to the application's “Additional Questions” field to confirm you have read the rules.

⏱️ How long do applications take?

Usually around two hours, but it can take up to 24 hours.

🌐 Is cross-play supported?

Yes! Java and Bedrock players can play together.

🧭 Does LuckyGem have /tpa, homes, or warps?

No. LuckyGem aims to stay as close to vanilla as possible, with a few additions that support the experience and help staff moderate the server.

🛟 How do I get help?

Open a support ticket in the LuckyGem Discord Info Hub and explain what you need. Staff will reply as soon as they can.

🌱 How do I become staff?

Apply through the Discord Info Hub when applications are available. Be helpful, respectful, and welcoming; please don't ask to be staff.

💚 What if I take a break?

Players are not removed from the whitelist for inactivity. If you'll be away and want your shop or base considered for protection, submit a support ticket with your expected absence.

🏆 What else happens here?

LuckyGem features vanilla-enhancing datapacks, 2,600+ advancements, weekly competitions, minigames, and community activities. The weekly competition Champion role lasts one month; another win adds a month to the remaining time.

💬 Where can I suggest ideas?

Use the suggestions forum on Discord. React to the onboarding prompts to unlock the available Discord channels.

🎁 Can I donate?

There is currently no donation system set up. Boosting the Discord server grants a Supporter role for as long as the boost lasts.

These website rules are an edited, easier-to-scan version of the rules provided by the LuckyGem team. The Discord rules and staff guidance remain the source of truth if anything changes.
